Meet the team before the quiz
CPU, the brain. Clock speed is how fast one worker goes. Cores are extra workers. A middle brain is enough for school and many games. A top brain finishes hard jobs faster and costs much more.
RAM, the desk. It holds what you are using right now and forgets when power goes off. Two matching sticks (dual channel) can share the path and feel quicker than one lonely stick. Memory costs more right now, so a huge desk can jump in price.
Motherboard, the map. The socket must match the CPU. The chipset decides extra roads, like how many fast storage slots you get.
Storage: HDD, SATA SSD, NVMe SSD. An HDD spins and is cheaper for old photos, but slower. A SATA SSD has no spin and uses a cable. An NVMe SSD plugs into a fast slot and usually opens games quickest. A middle NVMe is the usual home pick.
GPU, the artist, and FPS. The GPU draws games and video. FPS is pictures each second. About 30 can look jumpy, 60 looks smooth, and 120 looks extra smooth if the monitor can keep up. VRAM is the artist's own desk for textures. A small artist can live inside some CPUs. A card is a stronger artist. Higher resolution means more pixels, so the same card gets fewer FPS.
Power, case, and you. The power supply should cover the parts with spare room. The case needs airflow. Keyboard and mouse are how you talk. The screen only shows the picture.

Brands are shops with different strengths
Same shelf, different tools. Intel and AMD both make CPU brains. Nvidia, AMD, and Intel all make GPU artists. Compare the actual part, not just the logo.
CPU advantages. Intel is often easy to find and strong on one hard task plus everyday school and games. AMD often gives more cores for the money, which helps when you game and make videos together. Both have starter, middle, and pricey top chips. The board socket must match that brand.
GPU advantages. Nvidia is often the smoothest in big games and video tools, with helpful extras, and the top cards get very expensive. AMD is often the better game picture for the money. Intel cards are newer, usually cheaper, and a friendly pick for class and lighter games. A stronger card usually means higher FPS in the same game and resolution.
Series, balance, and price. Low series is the starter, middle is the sweet spot, and top looks best but the price jumps. A top artist with a tiny brain or a tiny power feeder wastes money. One nice part plus middle friends is the smart home build.
